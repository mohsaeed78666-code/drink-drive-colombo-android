FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Will the driver drive my own vehicle?

Yes. This is a designated driver service for your own vehicle. Confirm the trip details with our team when booking.

Is the service available 24/7?

You can contact us 24/7 to request a driver. Availability for a specific time and location is confirmed by our team.

How much does a trip cost?

The listed base rates are Rs. 2,500 for the first 10 km within the Colombo service area and Rs. 3,500 for the first 10 km for out-of-Colombo trips. Additional distance is charged at the rates shown above. Confirm the final quote before the trip.

How do I book?

Call 076 405 8871 or send a WhatsApp message with your pickup location, destination, and preferred time.
